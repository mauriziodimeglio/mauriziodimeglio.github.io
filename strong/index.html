Stay strong after 40

After 40, strength training is the foundation: three focused sessions a week can make a real difference. Supplements, if you use them, are optional extras — they do not replace training, a varied diet, or rest.

Three simple tips for staying strong after 40

  1. 1

    Lift three times a week

    A regular, full-body session is more useful than occasional marathon workouts. Start with loads you can control well, and increase them little by little.

  2. 2

    Respect recovery

    Leave at least one rest day between sessions. If you feel sharp or unusual pain, stop and talk to a doctor or a qualified health professional.

  3. 3

    Consistency beats intensity

    A short warm-up, a varied diet with enough protein, and adequate sleep will help more than pushing too hard, too often.
